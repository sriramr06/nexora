# Problem Identification

**Project:** Nexora — Multi-Tenant Project Management SaaS  
**Phase:** 1 — Product Discovery  
**Step:** 1.2 — Problem Identification  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Desk Research Completed — Pending User Validation

## 1. Objective

Identify problems that software development teams may encounter while coordinating project activities, managing tasks and monitoring project progress.

## 2. Identified Problems

The following problems have been selected as the primary areas of investigation.

| Priority | Problem | Description |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Poor task visibility | Limited visibility into task status, progress and blockers |
| P2 | Unclear task ownership | Unclear responsibility for assigned and pending tasks |
| P3 | Poor progress tracking | Difficulty monitoring project progress and identifying delays |

## 3. Problem Descriptions

### 3.1 Poor Task Visibility

Team members may struggle to understand which tasks are pending, in progress, completed or blocked.

This can make it difficult to understand the current state of project execution.

### 3.2 Unclear Task Ownership

When responsibilities are not clearly assigned or communicated, team members may be uncertain about who is responsible for particular tasks.

This can create coordination difficulties and delays.

### 3.3 Poor Progress Tracking

Without a clear overview of task completion and project status, team leads and project managers may struggle to identify delays and understand overall progress.

## 4. Refined Problem Statement

Software development teams often lack a centralized and transparent way to coordinate project tasks. This can lead to unclear task ownership, limited visibility into ongoing work and difficulties tracking overall project progress.

As a result, team members may struggle to understand responsibilities, identify delays and maintain a clear picture of project execution.

## 5. Research Methodology

**Selected approach:** Secondary Research (Desk Research).

Research references include:

1. Atlassian — State of Teams 2024.
2. Atlassian — State of Teams 2025.
3. Atlassian — State of Developer Experience 2025.

These reports provide supporting context about collaboration, information visibility and developer workflow challenges.

## 6. Validation Limitations

- No direct user interviews were conducted.
- No primary user survey was conducted.
- Research findings may not represent every software development team.
- Industry-wide findings cannot establish demand for this specific application.
- The three identified problems remain hypotheses for the selected audience.

## 7. Conclusion

Secondary research provides reasonable support for investigating task visibility, ownership clarity and project progress tracking.

The selected problems will guide the product vision and subsequent requirements definition.

However, they should be treated as preliminary problem hypotheses rather than fully validated user needs.
